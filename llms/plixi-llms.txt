# Plixi

> Plixi is an AI-powered Instagram growth platform for influencers, businesses, e-commerce brands and content creators. It grows accounts through paid advertising, micro-interactions and niche audience-data clustering, with a machine-learning targeting feature (AI-Match) users train by rating accounts. Plixi is a consumer/SMB SaaS product: as of 2026-08-13 it publishes **no public developer API**, no OpenAPI/GraphQL/MCP contract, and no developer portal.
